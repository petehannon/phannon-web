Favouritier

Recently moved to Apple Music, or just want to get your favourite artists in order?

Favouriting artists helps Apple Music tailor recommendations and keep you up to date with the music you care about, but Apple’s built-in migration doesn’t bring across your favourite artists, and adding them one by one can be painfully slow.

Enter Favouritier.

Choose a playlist, review the artists Favouritier finds, skip anyone you don’t want, and add the rest to your Apple Music favourites in just a few taps.

Why Favouritier?

  • Quickly tailor Apple Music to your tastes
  • Favourite artists from any playlist in bulk
  • See which artists are already favourites
  • Review everything before making changes
  • Native Apple Music integration
  • No accounts, no external logins, no tracking

Favouritier works directly with Apple Music. Your music data isn’t sent to an external service.
