Favouritier Privacy Policy

Favouritier does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

Favouritier uses Apple’s MusicKit framework to access Apple Music content you choose, identify artists in your playlists, check their favourite status, and make changes you explicitly request.

Favouritier does not operate a backend service. Your Apple Music data is not transmitted to or stored by the developer. Requests made through MusicKit are processed by Apple in accordance with Apple’s own privacy policies.

Favouritier does not use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or external account services.

You can revoke Favouritier’s access to Apple Music at any time through the privacy settings on your Apple device.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support@phannon.net
