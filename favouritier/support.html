Need help with Favouritier?

Email: support@phannon.net

Why does Favouritier need Apple Music access?

Favouritier needs access to identify artists in playlists, check which are already favourites, and favourite the artists you select.

Does Favouritier collect my music data?

No. Favouritier does not operate a backend and does not send your music data to the developer.

Why wasn't an artist included?

Favouritier identifies the primary artist associated with each track. Some collaborations, featured artists, or unusual catalogue entries may therefore produce different results.

How do I remove Apple Music access?

You can manage Favouritier’s Apple Music permission through your device's Privacy settings.
